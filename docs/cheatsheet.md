## Add a paper · 添加一篇论文

**EN:** Drag a paper (PDF, or a note with the link/DOI) into the `inbox` folder inside
`~/Notes/kdb`, then say **"ingest"**.
**中文：** 把一篇论文（PDF，或者带链接/DOI 的笔记）拖进 `~/Notes/kdb` 里的 `inbox`
文件夹，然后说 **"ingest"**。

**EN:** __ASSISTANT_NAME__ reads it, files it, and links it into your library. If it thinks
it's worth extracting graded claims from, it will ask you first.
**中文：** __ASSISTANT_NAME__ 会读它、归档，并把它链接进你的知识库。如果它觉得值得从中
提炼出可评级的结论，会先问你。

## Ask it things · 问问题

**EN:** Ask research questions in **either language** — English or 中文, whichever you're
more comfortable with. It answers in the same language you asked in.
**中文：** 用**任意一种语言**问研究相关的问题——中文或英文都可以，看你哪个顺手。它会用
你提问的那种语言回答你。

- "帮我总结一下我知识库里关于多囊卵巢综合征的内容。"

## Not medical advice · 不是医疗建议

**EN:** This library is research, not medical advice. Any claim that reaches a customer

**EN:** Once you start writing your own health pages, they are **yours alone** —

**EN:** __ASSISTANT_NAME__ can read your email and **draft** a reply — but it can never
send, delete, or trash anything. **You press send, in Gmail, every time.**

## If something looks stuck · 卡住了怎么办

**EN:** Quit the terminal (Cmd+Q), reopen it, and start again from the top. Your files and
drafts are all still there — it just doesn't remember what you were just talking about, so
say it again.
**中文：** 退出终端（Cmd+Q），重新打开，再从头开始。你的文件和草稿都还在——它只是不记得
你们刚才聊到哪儿了，再说一遍就行。
