## Basis

Base grade B (single adequately powered RCT — [[studies/cienfuegos-2020-4h-6h-trf]],
N = 58 randomized / 49 completers — per the rubric's "1 RCT" tier). A one-letter
population-adjustment drop to **C** applies here because `applies_to` names a
women-specific population (`women-with-obesity`) and the supporting study carries
`evidence_gap: women` — it was never sex-stratified, so despite the female-majority
sample, CLAUDE.md's rubric requires treating the evidence *for the women population
specifically* as one grade weaker than the pooled result alone would suggest. Contrast
[[claims/trf-weight-loss-adults]], which cites the same study for the general-adults
population and is not subject to this drop.

## Notes

- No study currently in this vault reports a sex-stratified TRF/insulin-resistance
  analysis — this is a genuine open evidence question, not just a labeling artifact of
  this schema.
- See [[conditions/pcos]] for a downstream condition where this same evidence gap
  (insulin resistance, glucose handling, in women) matters even more directly, and
  remains unaddressed by any trial in this vault.
