## Overview

Time-restricted eating (TRE) / time-restricted feeding (TRF) confines daily caloric
intake to a fixed daily window (e.g. 8 hours, or 4–6 hours) without necessarily reducing
total calories by design — though it often does so passively (see
[[claims/trf-weight-loss-adults]]'s Notes on spontaneous intake reduction in the
supporting trial). This vault currently holds two worked-example trials: one in
resistance-trained men ([[studies/moro-2016-trf-males]], narrative tier, male-only,
⚠️ evidence gap — women) and one in adults with obesity, ~90% female
([[studies/cienfuegos-2020-4h-6h-trf]], structured tier, two claims extracted,
⚠️ evidence gap — women — the cohort was mostly female but the results were never
sex-stratified).

**Women-specific RCT evidence is thin across both trials here** — the male-only study
excludes women entirely, and the majority-female study never isolates a sex-specific
effect. See [[interventions/16-8-fasting]]'s "Women specifically" subsection and
[[conditions/pcos]] for where this gap compounds with a female-specific endocrine
condition.
